## A. Education and DepEd words

| Word | Plain meaning |
|---|---|
| **DepEd** | Department of Education of the Philippines. |
| **MATATAG Curriculum** | The revised basic education curriculum, DepEd Order No. 10, s. 2024. Written grade by grade; every competency has a code, like **RL1PWS-I-5**. Our first check texts, mistake patterns and activities carry these codes. |
| **Competency code (for example RL1PWS-I-5)** | A label for one skill the curriculum teaches. Read it as: subject and grade (RL1 = Reading and Literacy, Grade 1), the strand (PWS = Phonics and Word Study), a quarter or domain, and a number. RL1PWS-I-5 means "sound out words accurately". Confirm each in the curriculum guide. |
| **Key Stage 1** | Kindergarten to Grade 3, the foundation stage. |
| **Phil-IRI** | Philippine Informal Reading Inventory. DepEd's oral reading test for finding a learner's reading level. Policy: DepEd Order No. 14, s. 2018. |
| **Independent level** | Reads this level comfortably alone: 97 percent or more of words right in one passage. |
| **Instructional level** | Learns at this level with a teacher's guidance: 90 to 96 percent. |
| **Frustration level** | Too hard even with help: under 90 percent. Only teachers see it; children and parents see the four steps instead. |
| **Non-reader** | Cannot yet recognize and sound out letters and simple words. |
| **Hamon: Bawat Bata Bumabasa** | "Challenge: Every Child a Reader". A DepEd reading programme (Memorandum No. 173, s. 2019). |
| **Six elements of reading** | Oral language, phonological awareness, phonics, vocabulary, fluency, comprehension. |
| **Decoding** | Turning printed letters into sounds and words. |
| **Phonological awareness** | Hearing and playing with the sounds in spoken words, including syllables. |
| **Phonics** | Linking letters to sounds to read words. |
| **Fluency** | Reading with accuracy, a good pace and expression. |
| **Prosody** | The expression and rhythm of reading aloud. Our reading service gives a rough score; children never see it. |
| **Comprehension** | Understanding what was read. Our quiz is only for the comprehension-type activities. |
| **Oral reading accuracy** | The share of words read correctly aloud. |
| **WCPM** | Words correct per minute, a speed measure. We call it "words a minute" on screens. |
| **Syllable** | A beat in a word: sun-set has two. |
| **Closed syllable** | A syllable that ends with a consonant and has a short vowel (sun, set, bas, ket). We start the syllable method with these. |
| **CVC** | Consonant, vowel, consonant: cat, dog, sun. |
| **Blend** | Two or three consonants said together: fr in frog. |
| **Sight words** | Common words read at a glance (the, was). |
| **Gradual release of responsibility** | "I do, we do, you do": the teacher shows, then they do it together, then the child alone. Our two free practice tries follow it. |
| **Guided reading / small group** | Teaching a small group at their own instructional level. Our reading groups support this. |
| **Multigrade class (DepEd)** | One teacher teaches two or more grades together (DepEd Orders 96 s. 1997 and 81 s. 2009). We do **not** model it: one class, one grade. |
| **School head** | The principal, who assigns teaching loads and class programs. This is why an added grade needs Admin confirmation. |
| **MOOE** | Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses, a school-level fund. Whether it can pay for software is **to be validated**. |
| **SEF** | Special Education Fund, a local government fund. Same caution. |

## E. The numbers on one page

**Basis key:** D = DepEd document; T = team setting (in one settings file); S = a standard from research or design.

### The reading levels (D)
| One reading | Name |
|---|---|
| 97 percent or more | Independent |
| 90 to 96 | Instructional |
| under 90 | Frustration |

### Placement and movement (T)
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| First check length | at most 3 texts; 7 rungs; 21 curated texts (3 per rung) |
| First check moves | 90 or more up; under 70 down; 70 to 89 stop; **letters up at 80** |
| Move up | 3 readings of 90 or more, from 2 activities, in the last 5 since the last move |
| Move down | 2 readings under 70 in a row, on texts not too long for the child |
| Words needed to count | Letters 4, Words 6, Short sentences 10, Sentences 17, Short stories 27, Stories 45 |
| Steps | 7 rungs folded into 4 steps |

### Scoring (T)
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| "Not sure" word | confidence under 0.5; at most 25 percent of words |
| Letter names | repaired only where the service marked wrong; adds credit only |
| Points | about half the accuracy percent (80 percent gives 40) |

### Patterns and alerts (T)
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Mistake pattern | 3 readings, 6 misses, 25 percent of misses; "early" until 5 readings; 10 kinds |
| Needs support | latest reading under 70, within 14 days |
| Ready to move up | last 3 readings at 90 or more, not used for a move |
| Gone quiet | 7 days or more |
| Class fact for the AI note | at least 2 children |
| "Did it work?" | at least 3 children read both kinds; "about the same" under 3 points |

### Length check "can this child read it" (T), words: comfortable / stretch
Letters 6 / 10; Words 10 / 14; Short sentences 16 / 24; Sentences 28 / 40; Short stories 45 / 60; Stories 75 / 100; Longer stories 130 / 170. A whole class is refused at 20 percent.

### Easy, Medium, Hard word counts (from the activity writer)
Examples. Word list, Grade 1: 5 to 7 / 8 to 10 / 11 to 14; Grade 3: 8 to 10 / 11 to 14 / 15 to 20. Passage, Grade 1: 20 to 30 / 31 to 45 / 46 to 65; Grade 3: 40 to 60 / 61 to 90 / 91 to 130.

### The recommender (T, service version 1.0.0)
Foundational: 0.80 accuracy + 0.20 speed. Fluency: 0.40 accuracy + 0.35 speed + 0.25 prosody. Comprehension: quiz score. New score = 0.70 old + 0.30 attempt. Easy up to 59, medium up to 79, hard above. Confidence starts at 0.60, plus 0.08 per attempt, up to 0.95.
Worked example: 87.5 x 0.40 + 100 x 0.35 + 55 x 0.25 = 83.75; 97.4 x 0.70 + 83.75 x 0.30 = 93.3.

### Games and word bank (T)
Round of 3 things; 2 tries each; 2 right first time to climb; up to 3 rounds; "heard" at 66 percent; 4 games. Word bank: Mastered after 3 correct readings; at most 4 new words per reading. Syllable method: 27 words, 4 steps, only the last scored.

### Rewards (T)
100 badges in 10 groups; weekly goal 5 real readings; streak = days in a row (Philippine time).

### Safety limits (T)
Sign in: 5 tries then wait. PIN reset: 3 per child per day. Class code lookup: 20 refusals an hour. Cleaning: recordings deleted after scoring.

### Design numbers (S)
Tap target 48 points (phone) or 44 pixels (web). Passage text 23 pixels or more. Five text sizes.

### The size of the work
4 user roles; 775 automated tests passing (23 October 2026); 100 badges; 10 mistake patterns; 4 games; 7 rungs; 4 child steps; 3 recommender skills; 3 outside AI services; 1 hosting provider; 1 database.

### Money (file 04)
Cash cost for a steady school about USD 48 to 55 a month (about PHP 3,000 at 58 per USD, an assumption); example school fee PHP 1,500 a month; break-even two schools at that price.

## F. Words to avoid, and what to say instead

| Do not say | Say |
|---|---|
| "Our AI is trained on children's voices." | "We use a pretrained speech service and rules we wrote; nothing was trained." |
| "It is accurate." | "It is forgiving by design and we measured these specific cases." |
| "DepEd says 90 and 70." | "DepEd's bands are 97 and 90; the 70 and the three-reading rule are our settings." |
| "It was tested on children." | "It was tested with automated tests and computer voices; a consented pilot is next." |
| "It improves reading." | "It is built to support reading; improvement needs a pilot to show." |
| "Approved by DepEd." | "Aligned to DepEd documents; not made or endorsed by the Department of Education." |
| "The AI decides." | "Rules decide, a teacher approves, and the evidence is always shown." |
| "It is secure." | "We did a security audit, fixed what it found, and here is what remains open." |
| "We will make money." | "Here is the cost, the plan, and what we will test first." |
