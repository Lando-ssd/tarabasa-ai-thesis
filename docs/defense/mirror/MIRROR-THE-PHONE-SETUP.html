Show the phone app on the laptop

Setup guide for the teammate who handles the app. About 15 minutes, done once. Then rehearse the whole demo this way before the defense.

Why we need this Our defense instructions say every group must mirror its mobile app onto a laptop during the presentation, so the panel can see the phone screen on the projector. We use scrcpy, a free open source tool. It works over a USB cable, so it does not depend on the venue Wi-Fi. The group chat also lists a second tool, Screen Mirror (screenmirrorapp.com). We have not tried that one; use it only if scrcpy does not work on your phone.

0What you need

  • An Android phone with TaraBasa AI 0.6.4 installed (the file TaraBasa-AI-0.6.4-test24.apk). An iPhone cannot be used: the app is Android only.
  • A USB cable that carries data. Some cables only charge. If the laptop does not see the phone, try another cable first.
  • A Windows 10 or 11 laptop with a free USB port (a Mac works too, see the note at the end).
  • Internet on the phone (Wi-Fi or mobile data), so the app can reach the live site.

1Get scrcpy on the laptop (about 5 minutes)

  1. Open the official page: https://github.com/Genymobile/scrcpy/releases/tag/v4.1
  2. Scroll down to Assets and download scrcpy-win64-v4.1.zip (about 11 MB). On an old 32 bit laptop, use scrcpy-win32-v4.1.zip.
  3. Right click the zip, choose Extract All, and put the folder somewhere simple, like C:\scrcpy. There is nothing to install.
  4. Open the folder. You should see scrcpy.exe, adb.exe and some .dll files.
If Windows or your antivirus complains Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. It is a well known open source tool from its official page. If your antivirus quietly removes a file, restore it and allow it.

Shortcut: the owner's laptop already has an earlier copy (version 4.0) in Downloads\scrcpy-win64-v4.0. It works the same, and the folder can be copied to a USB stick.

2Prepare the phone (about 3 minutes, once)

  1. Install the app. Copy the .apk to the phone and open it. If it says "install blocked", allow installs from that app (Files, Drive or Chrome) when asked. It installs over any older TaraBasa app.
  2. Turn on Developer options. Settings, About phone, then tap Build number seven times. (On Samsung: About phone, Software information, Build number. On Xiaomi or Redmi: tap the MIUI or OS version seven times.) Type the phone PIN if it asks.
  3. Turn on USB debugging. Settings, System (or Additional settings), Developer options, switch on USB debugging.
  4. Keep the screen awake. Settings, Display, Screen timeout, choose the longest time.

3Connect and mirror (about 2 minutes)

  1. Plug the phone into the laptop with the cable.
  2. On the phone a box asks "Allow USB debugging?" Tick "Always allow from this computer" and tap Allow. If the phone shows a USB mode menu, choose File transfer.
  3. On the laptop, open the scrcpy folder and double click scrcpy.exe. A window opens that shows the phone screen. You can click and type in it with the mouse and keyboard.
  4. For the projector, press Alt + F for full screen, and again to leave it.
  5. To stop, close the scrcpy window or unplug the cable.
Better way to start it (recommended) Open the scrcpy folder, click the address bar at the top, type cmd and press Enter. A black window opens. Type:
scrcpy --stay-awake --window-title "TaraBasa AI"
--stay-awake stops the phone from falling asleep while it is plugged in. Other options you may want:
  • --no-audio if the room has an echo or the app seems to hear the laptop speakers while a child reads. The phone then plays its own sound.
  • --max-size 1280 if the laptop is slow or the picture lags.
  • --record demo.mp4 to save the mirrored screen as a video. Use this to make the backup video (part 5).
On Android 11 or newer, scrcpy sends the phone's sound (Tara's voice and the read aloud) to the laptop. Test the sound and the microphone together in the real room, because the app listens to the reader.

4Demo day checklist

About 10 minutes before your turn

  • Open https://tarabasa-ai.onrender.com in a browser, so the free site wakes up. It sleeps when nobody uses it.
  • Do one short practice reading in the app a few minutes before. The speech service also sleeps, and the first reading can take 30 to 40 seconds to answer.
  • Phone: Do Not Disturb on, notifications off, battery above 60 percent (the cable charges it), screen timeout long, brightness high, rotation locked to portrait.
  • Sign in to the accounts you will use before presenting, so nobody types a PIN or password on the big screen. The logins are not in this file. The owner sends them privately.
  • Laptop plugged in, other programs and browser tabs closed, sound volume tested.
  • Rehearse the full demo once through the mirror. Follow the 40 minute demo script in the Defense Script and Study Guide.

5Backup plan

  • Record one perfect run beforehand with scrcpy --record demo.mp4 (stop it with Ctrl + C in the black window). Keep the video on the laptop and on a USB stick. Also keep a few screenshots from the phone.
  • If the mirror fails on the day, show the website on the laptop instead: https://tarabasa-ai.onrender.com. It has the same Learner, Teacher and Parent screens, with the same demo accounts.
  • If the internet fails, turn on the phone's mobile data hotspot and join it from the laptop, or use mobile data on the phone.

6If something goes wrong

What you seeTry this
"no devices found", or scrcpy cannot find the phoneUnplug and plug in again. Try another cable (a charge only cable will not work) and another USB port. Pull down the phone's notifications and set the USB mode to File transfer. Check that USB debugging is on.
"device unauthorized"Unplug, plug in, and tap Allow on the phone. If no box appears, go to Developer options, tap Revoke USB debugging authorizations, and plug in again.
The window is black or frozenClose scrcpy, plug in again, and start it again. Make sure the phone screen is on and unlocked.
Windows does not recognize the phone at allInstall the phone maker's USB driver (Samsung, Xiaomi and others have one), or try another USB port.
Slow or laggy picturePlug the cable straight into the laptop, not into a hub. Add --max-size 1024. Close other programs.
No sound on the laptopSound forwarding needs Android 11 or newer. On an older phone, use the phone's own speaker. Also check the laptop volume.
The app says it cannot reach TaraBasaThe phone has no internet, or the site is still waking up. Wait a minute and try again, and check Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Mac laptop: download scrcpy-macos-aarch64-v4.1.tar.gz (Apple chip) or scrcpy-macos-x86_64-v4.1.tar.gz (Intel) from the same page, extract it, open Terminal in that folder and run ./scrcpy. The phone steps are the same.
