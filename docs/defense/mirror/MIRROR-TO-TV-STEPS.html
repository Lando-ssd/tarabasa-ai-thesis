Show the phone on the TV

Do the steps in order. About 10 minutes the first time, about 3 minutes after that.

Phone
USB cable
Laptop
Wireless, or HDMI
TV

The defense instructions ask us to mirror the app onto a laptop. The TV is the second step. The phone needs internet for the app.

Bring: the Android phone with TaraBasa AI (charged), a USB cable that carries data (some only charge), the laptop and its charger, and the TV remote.

Part 1. Put the laptop on the TV with no cable

  1. 1On the TV, open Screen Mirroring (also called Screen Share or Miracast). Look in the Source or Apps menu. Leave that screen open.
  2. 2On the laptop turn Wi-Fi on, press Windows key + K, wait for the TV's name and click it. Say yes on the TV if it asks.
  3. 3Press Windows key + P and choose Duplicate.
  4. 4Sound: click the speaker icon at the bottom right, open the list of sound outputs and choose the TV.
TV not in the list? For a Google TV or Android TV, open Google Chrome, click the three dots, Cast, save, and share, Cast, open Sources, choose Cast screen, then the TV (same Wi-Fi).
HDMI port after all? An Acer Aspire A315-59 usually has one on its left side. A cable is steadier: plug it in, pick it with the TV's Source button, then do steps 3 and 4.
Nothing reaches the TV? Show the laptop screen. The app is still mirrored onto a laptop.

Part 2. Put the phone on the laptop

First time only, on the phone
  1. 5Settings, About phone, tap Build number seven times. Samsung: About phone, Software information. Xiaomi or Redmi: tap the MIUI or OS version.
  2. 6Settings, Developer options, switch on USB debugging. Xiaomi or Redmi: also switch on USB debugging (Security settings), or the mouse will not work.
Every time
  1. 7Plug the phone into the laptop. On the phone, tick Always allow from this computer and tap Allow.
  2. 8On the laptop open the scrcpy folder. On the owner's laptop: Downloads, then scrcpy-win64-v4.0, then the folder with the same name inside it. On another laptop, download it first (see the laptop setup guide).
  3. 9Click the address bar of that folder window, type cmd, press Enter. In the black window type this and press Enter:
    scrcpy --stay-awake --window-title "TaraBasa AI"
    Double clicking scrcpy.exe also works, but the phone may then lock itself.
  4. 10The phone appears in a window. Press Alt + F for full screen. Black bars on the left and right are normal because a phone is tall. Press Alt + F again to leave. Close the window to stop.

Part 3. Prove it works (5 minutes, watch the TV)

  • Touch the phone: the TV follows right away.
  • Open TaraBasa AI and sign in with a demo account before people watch, so no password or PIN shows on the TV.
  • Open a story and tap Listen: the voice comes out of the TV.
  • Do one reading: tap the microphone (allow it the first time), read, stop. A result appears.
  • Play one game, for example Sound Hunt.
  • Wait 3 minutes: the phone does not lock and the picture stays.
  • Unplug the phone, plug it back and get the picture again.
  • Turn on Do Not Disturb on the phone and the laptop, so no message shows on the TV.
All eight ticked means it works. Write down how you connected and which laptop and cable you used, and do it the same way in the defense room.

If something goes wrong

What you seeDo this
TV not in the Windows + K listKeep the TV on its Screen Mirroring screen. Check laptop Wi-Fi is on. Restart the TV. Press Windows + K again and wait one minute.
TV picture lags or dropsMove the laptop close to the TV. Close other programs. If it keeps dropping, show the laptop screen instead.
Picture on the TV is cut off or tinySet the TV picture size to "Screen fit" or "Just scan". Or press Windows + P, choose Extend, drag the phone window onto the TV and press Alt + F.
"Could not find any ADB device"Try another USB cable and port. Pull down the phone's notifications and set USB to File transfer. Check that USB debugging is on.
"unauthorized"Unlock the phone and tap Allow. If no box appears: Developer options, Revoke USB debugging authorizations, plug in again.
Picture works, mouse clicks do nothingXiaomi or Redmi: see step 6. Or simply touch the phone; the TV still follows.
Sound comes from the laptopChoose the TV as the sound output (step 4).
No sound from the TV or the laptopSound sharing needs Android 11 or newer (on Android 11 unlock the phone before starting). Or add --no-audio to the command, so the phone plays its own sound.
The app cannot hear the readerLower the TV volume while reading and read close to the phone. The app listens with the phone's microphone.
Picture lagsPlug straight into the laptop, not a hub. Add --max-size 1024 to the command.
App cannot reach TaraBasaCheck the phone's internet. The free server sleeps: open tarabasa-ai.onrender.com a few minutes early and wait a minute.

Backup 1. Phone straight to the TV, no cables

Only if the laptop way fails. The TV needs screen mirroring or Chromecast built in. Put both on the same Wi-Fi. Samsung: swipe down twice, tap Smart View, pick the TV. Other Android phones: swipe down and tap Cast, Screen cast or Wireless projection, pick the TV. Allow it on the TV. It can lag or drop in a busy room, so test it before you rely on it.

Backup 2. A recorded video

When it all works, record one perfect run with the command below, and close the phone window to stop. The video demo.mp4 is saved in the scrcpy folder. Play it once to check, then copy it to a USB stick too.

scrcpy --stay-awake --record demo.mp4
