What this page is
  • Each screen is the website's own page, filled with sample data and saved as it looks at that moment.
  • The phone frame draws a browser address bar and the phone's status bar and gesture bar, so the space matches a real phone.
  • Laptop shows the same pages at 1280 x 800, to compare with the phone layout.
  • Drag and drop between levels needs a mouse. On a phone the "Move to" menu on each card does the same job.
