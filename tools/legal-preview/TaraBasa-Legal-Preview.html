What changed

New

  • Five pages in one layout: Privacy Policy (rewritten), Terms of Use, Cookie Policy, Refund Policy and Parent and Guardian Consent. Each has a short version at the top, numbered sections, a version and a date.
  • People now tick a box to agree, and the system writes down who agreed, to what, which version of the pages, and when.
  • Fonts are served from TaraBasa's own site, so no visitor's address is sent to Google.

Where consent is asked

  • Parent sign up and Teacher sign up: agree to the Terms and read the Privacy Policy.
  • Add a child, last step: the parent confirms they are the child's parent or guardian. A photo has its own separate box.
  • Link to an existing child: a second guardian confirms they are the child's parent or guardian.

Not changed

  • Reading, scoring, levels, games and the Teacher screens work as before.
