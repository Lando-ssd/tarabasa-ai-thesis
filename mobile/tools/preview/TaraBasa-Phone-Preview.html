What changed in this layout pass
  • One spacing ladder (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 40) for every gap, padding and margin.
  • Every tap target is at least 48dp, also on the smallest phones.
  • The six letter tiles share the width three to a row, so none falls off the screen.
  • The warm-up and its "I did not hear anything" state fit a 640 tall phone without scrolling.
  • On most phones Tara's head and the microphone step down one size, so the story and the pinned microphone fit together. On the smallest phones Tara steps aside while a child reads.
  • Balloon Pop packs its word and "hear it" button side by side and shrinks the balloons a little on short phones.
  • A thin line appears above the pinned button only when the story scrolls beneath it.
